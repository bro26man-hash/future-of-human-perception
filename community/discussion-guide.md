# Community Discussion Guide

## For Listeners & Collaborators

### What Makes a Good Episode?
- **Real debate, not echo chambers** — We want multiple perspectives on every topic
- **Actionable takeaways** — What should a developer *do* differently after listening?
- **Human stories** — The person behind the code, the moment they felt presence break
- **Cross-disciplinary bridges** — Connect engineering to psychology, design, and accessibility

### How to Contribute

#### Suggest a Topic
Open a GitHub Discussion or Issue with:
- The question you want explored
- Related repositories or spec issues
- Potential guests (with links to their work)

#### Suggest a Guest
Found someone whose work connects perception and technology? 
Create a new issue with the `guest-suggestion` label.

#### Volunteer for Research
Each episode has a `research-needed` tag. Claim an issue and dive into the source material.

### Community Norms
- Be respectful of different perspectives on XR's future
- Cite sources — link to GitHub issues, spec PRs, papers
- No promotional content in discussions
- Welcome to all backgrounds — XR needs diverse voices
