## How to Approach Guests

1. **Start with their current work** — Reference the specific GitHub issue they're authoring
2. **Frame questions around perceptual science** — Connect their engineering work to how humans perceive the result
3. **Offer co-creation** — Let them shape the episode; this is a collaborative project
4. **Be mindful of time zones** — Most contributors are in US/EU; schedule accordingly
5. **Follow up on low-activity repos** — Some contributors may be waiting for a compelling invitation
